# Content Review Squad

**Commercial-grade review intelligence for product and CX teams.**

Content Review Squad turns messy customer feedback into clean, prioritized action: bug tickets, feature specs, and marketing-ready testimonials. It pairs multi-agent AI with human approval and audit logging so teams can scale review processing without losing control.

---

## Why it matters
- **Scale:** 10k–100k reviews/month without expanding the ops team.
- **Speed:** Automated triage + structured outputs cut the time-to-decision.
- **Confidence:** Human-in-the-loop approvals for high-impact changes.
- **Accountability:** Audit log keeps every decision traceable.

---

## What it does
- **Auto-triage** reviews into Bugs, Features, Praise (with confidence & language).
- **Generate structured outputs**: bug reports, feature specs, testimonials.
- **Prioritize** feature requests with impact/effort scoring + frequency boost.
- **Cluster & dedupe** similar topics to prevent decision noise.
- **Queue for review** when confidence is low or approval is required.
- **Export** JSON/CSV for BI, ticketing, or downstream systems.

---

## Key capabilities
- Multi-agent workflow (triage → specialist agents → summary).
- Human-in-the-loop approvals for feature specs.
- Audit log for every decision and action taken.
- KPI summary: volume, confidence, severity/priority mix, approval rate.
- Global-language support (language detection + multilingual reviews).
- Evaluation suite to track accuracy and data quality over time.

---

- **Faster roadmap decisions:** clear, ranked feature demand.
- **Cleaner bug pipeline:** actionable, consistent bug reports.
- **Marketing lift:** high-quality testimonials extracted automatically.
- **Operational clarity:** review queue + audit log for compliance.
